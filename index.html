How to Calculate Your Exact Age Manually

Start by subtracting your birth year from the current year — that's your base age. If your birthday hasn't happened yet this year, subtract one more year, since you haven't had that birthday. Do the same comparison for the month and day: if today's day-of-month is earlier than your birth day-of-month, borrow a month (and add that month's number of days to the day count), the same way you'd borrow a ten when subtracting numbers by hand.

Leap years complicate this slightly, since February has 29 days every four years. This calculator handles that automatically by working with actual calendar dates rather than a fixed 365-day assumption, so the result stays accurate regardless of how many leap years fall within your lifetime.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this age calculator private?

Yes. All calculations run entirely client-side in your browser using JavaScript. Your date of birth is never sent to a server or stored anywhere.

How do I calculate my exact age manually?

Subtract your birth year from the current year to get your base age in years, then adjust for whether your birthday has occurred yet this year. Do the same for months and days, borrowing from the next-higher unit when the subtraction goes negative, similar to regular subtraction with carrying.
